Designed and ran three original experimental studies: cold-start behavior of the model's interpretability scores, the empirical semantics of its three internal prediction streams, and assumption-breaking tests based on controlled edits to student response sequences. Showed that the model's named interpretability scores are largely redundant, and that its explanations are unstable during a student's early interactions even though predictive accuracy (AUC) remains stable. Built a pipeline that generates teacher-facing explanations from the model's internal scores and validates LLM-generated explanations through hallucination checks, faithfulness grading against measured correlation ceilings, and forbidden-claim detection. Reproduced the MSKT metacognitive knowledge tracing model on the EdNet-KT4 dataset, obtaining results close to those reported in the original paper. Independently identified an implementation inconsistency in the published QIKT source code and overparameterization in one of its modules.
